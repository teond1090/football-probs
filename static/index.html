How this works

Pinnacle takes the largest bets in the world at the smallest margin, so its prices with the margin removed are the best available estimate of each outcome's true chance. When another sportsbook pays more than that fair price at the same line, the bet has positive expected value. This doesn't depend on the app's own model.

Edges are small (usually 1–5%), so results only show up over many bets. Bet the suggested stake, track every bet, and check your record after 100+. Edges over 10% are flagged verify: they're usually a line the book is about to move. Sportsbooks may limit accounts that win consistently.
